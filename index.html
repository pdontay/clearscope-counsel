// Billable hours, meet the modern era

Pay for the plan, not the clock.

The billable hour rewards time spent, not problems solved. Which is a strange incentive to hand the person you're trusting with your future.

The old way

Billing by the minute

  • Priced by time spent, not the problem solved
  • The longer it takes, the more you pay
  • Itemized invoices that arrive after the work
  • You quietly help fund the learning curve
  • The meter runs during "quick questions"
The modern way

Flat fees

  • Priced around value and a defined scope
  • Efficiency lowers your cost instead of raising it
  • One agreed number, known before we start
  • AI handles the repetitive drafting and research
  • Ask questions without watching a timer
An hourly invoice can bill you for the coffee refill and the slow walk back from the printer. A flat fee bills you for one thing: the result you came for.AI-assisted is not AI-decided: a licensed attorney reviews, and is responsible for, your work.
